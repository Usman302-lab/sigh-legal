Privacy policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

Short version: if you use Sigh without an account, your sessions stay on your device. If you sign in, your sessions are backed up to your own Supabase account. We do not sell data, and we do not show personalised ads.

1. Who we are

Sigh is a breathwork app. For privacy requests contact usmanarshadjutt302@gmail.com. We are based in Pakistan.

2. Guest mode

Without an account, Sigh stores sessions, settings and programme progress in private app storage on your device. This data is not transmitted to us. We do not collect analytics or contact data in guest mode. The free tier still requests one banner ad from Google AdMob, which may process device information as described in section 5; Pro removes ads.

3. If you sign in

Sign-in is optional and uses Google or Apple. We receive your email address, display name and avatar URL from the provider. We store your sessions — pattern, duration, self-reported mood before and after, optional note, programme day and completion time — in Supabase, associated with your user id. We do not receive your contacts, precise location, photos, health-platform data or advertising identifier.

4. Purchases

Apple or Google processes payments. RevenueCat validates receipts and tells the app whether Pro is active. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user id, purchase history and basic device/OS information; it does not receive your session notes.

5. Ads

The free tier shows one banner ad on the Today screen, provided by Google AdMob. In this version ads are requested in non-personalised mode; we do not use the advertising identifier for personalised advertising and do not present an App Tracking Transparency prompt. AdMob may still process device information, IP address and approximate location to serve and measure ads, as described in Google's policies. Pro removes ads.

6. Processors and transfers

  • Supabase — authentication and cloud session storage.
  • RevenueCat — subscription entitlement.
  • Google AdMob — advertising on the free tier.
  • Apple / Google — payments and platform services.

These providers may process data outside your country; transfers rely on appropriate safeguards such as Standard Contractual Clauses where required.

7. Legal bases (UK/EU GDPR)

  • Contract: providing the account, backup and Pro features.
  • Consent: optional sign-in, optional notifications and optional cloud sync.
  • Legitimate interests: security, fraud prevention, basic product operation.

Mood and session data can be health-adjacent. When you sign in you explicitly choose to store it in your cloud account; you can delete it at any time.

8. Retention

Local data remains until you delete it or uninstall the app. Cloud sessions remain until you delete them or your account. Purchase records are kept by the stores and RevenueCat under their policies. Support emails are deleted after 24 months.

9. Your rights and choices

  • Export: Settings → Your data → Export sessions (JSON).
  • Delete local data: Settings → Your data → Delete local data.
  • Delete account: Settings → Account → Delete account and all data. This removes the auth user and cloud sessions. You can also use our web deletion instructions if you cannot open the app.
  • Notifications: optional; you choose during onboarding and can change them at any time in Settings or your device settings.
  • Depending on your jurisdiction you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict or object, and to complain to a supervisory authority.

10. Security

Data in transit uses TLS. Cloud rows are protected by row-level security so a user can only read or write their own sessions. Local data uses the operating system's app sandbox. Android auto-backup is disabled. No method is perfectly secure; protect your device passcode and exported files.

11. Children

Sigh is intended for adults aged 18 and over and is not directed at children.

12. Changes

If we change how data is handled we will update this policy and, for material changes, ask for consent in the app.
