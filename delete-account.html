Delete your Sigh account

Last updated: 10 November 2026

In the app: Settings → Account & backup → Delete account and all data. This immediately removes your Sigh auth user, profile and cloud sessions.

Delete from the app (fastest)

  1. Open Sigh and go to Settings.
  2. Under Account & backup, tap Delete account and all data.
  3. Confirm DELETE EVERYTHING.
  4. Sigh signs you out, deletes the cloud account and returns to onboarding.

If you cannot open the app

Email privacy@sigh.app from the Google or Apple email address used for Sigh, with the subject “Delete my Sigh account”. We may ask you to verify control of that address. We will complete the deletion within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your Supabase authentication user.
  • Your profile row (display name and account metadata).
  • All cloud sessions, including pattern, duration, mood, notes and programme progress.
  • Local sessions and settings on the device when you use the in-app deletion or uninstall the app.

What is not deleted by Sigh

  • Store purchase records held by Apple or Google, and RevenueCat’s entitlement records, are retained under their policies for accounting, fraud prevention and restore-purchase purposes.
  • An active subscription is not cancelled by deleting your Sigh account. Cancel it in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions, or Apple ID → Subscriptions.
  • Records we must keep for legal, tax or security reasons, for the period the law requires.

Guest data

If you never signed in, there is no cloud account to delete. Use Settings → Your data → Delete local data, or uninstall the app, to remove local sessions and settings.
