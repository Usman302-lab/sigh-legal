Delete your Sigh data

Last updated: 12 November 2026

You can delete some or all of your data without deleting your Sigh account. Use the in-app option for local data, or email us for cloud data.

Delete local data in the app

  1. Open Sigh and go to Settings.
  2. Scroll to Your data.
  3. Tap Delete local data and confirm.

This removes sessions, mood reflections, custom patterns, settings and programme progress stored on that device. It does not delete your account, and it does not delete data already synced to the cloud.

Request deletion of cloud data

Email usmanarshadjutt302@gmail.com from the Google or Apple email address used for Sigh, with the subject “Delete my Sigh data”. Tell us whether you want:

  • all cloud data deleted, or
  • only specific data, such as sessions, mood reflections, custom patterns, programme progress or settings.

We may ask you to verify control of the account email. We will complete the request within 30 days. You do not need to delete your account.

Data that is deleted

  • Session records, including pattern, duration, mood, notes and timestamps.
  • Mood reflections and weekly reflections.
  • Custom breathing patterns.
  • Programme and course progress.
  • App settings and preferences stored in the cloud.
  • Local copies on the device when you use Delete local data or uninstall Sigh.

Data that is kept

  • Account details — your email address, display name and authentication record remain until you delete your account. You can delete the account separately.
  • Purchase and subscription records — Google Play, the App Store and RevenueCat retain records needed for accounting, fraud prevention, tax and restore-purchase purposes.
  • Legal and security records — retained only for the period required by law.
  • Backups — deleted data may remain in encrypted backups for up to 90 days before those backups expire.

Delete your whole account

If you want the account and all associated data removed, use Delete your Sigh account, or go to Settings → Account & backup → Delete account and all data. An active subscription is not cancelled by deleting your account; cancel it in Google Play or the App Store.
